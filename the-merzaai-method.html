A deliberate approach

Clarity is not a single moment. It is a process.

FRAME DISTILL RESOLVE DESIGN COMMIT

Every organisation faces moments when the situation is complicated, the priorities are competing, or the path forward is difficult to see. Moving quickly without understanding the problem can create more activity without creating meaningful progress.

The Merzaai Method gives that work a structure. It brings together discovery, analysis, decision-making, solution design and commitment in one connected process.

Each stage builds on the one before it, helping turn an unclear situation into a practical direction that people can act on.

Better outcomes begin with better understanding.

Five connected stages

Every stage answers a different question.

The process is designed to bring discipline to complex situations without losing sight of the people and decisions involved.

01

FRAME

Understand the situation

Understand the context, define the challenge and establish what really needs to change.

Before solving anything, we need to understand what is happening. FRAME establishes the context, clarifies the objectives and defines the challenge that needs attention.

02

DISTILL

Find what matters

Find the important signals, uncover root causes and separate facts from assumptions.

Complex situations often contain more information than is useful. DISTILL examines the available information, identifies patterns, tests assumptions and brings the underlying issues into focus.

AnalysisKey findingsRoot causesInsights
03

RESOLVE

Make clear decisions

Establish priorities, evaluate choices and determine the direction that creates the greatest value.

Insight needs direction. RESOLVE turns findings into choices. It establishes what deserves attention, considers trade-offs and creates alignment around the decisions that matter most.

Once the direction is clear, DESIGN turns it into something practical. This stage develops the solution, defines the operating approach and maps out the steps needed to move forward.
