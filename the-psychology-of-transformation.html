The governing philosophy

Transformation moves through choices.

Focus: the question we ask decides the movement we get.

×

How do we change the environment?

Not the question.

×

How do we force the organisation to change?

Not the question.

→

What choices are available now?

Instead, ask this.

→

What will we decide?

And this.

Change is not one commitment. It is a sequence of choices that create or prevent movement.

04Lens 4

The Ambiguity Cloud

When people cannot see the important path, they retreat toward work they can control.

Not every uncertainty needs more information. Some uncertainties need a decision.

Clear the cloud by separating uncertainty into four types:

Fact

Supported by evidence

Assumption

Accepted temporarily

Decision

Requires a choice

Unknown

Requires investigation

05Lens 5

Horizon Stretch

Transformation quality declines when the decision horizon is shorter than the consequence horizon.

The safest decision for the decision-maker is not always the safest decision for the business.

Test every material design choice across:

Now · Horizon 1

Immediate value and pressure

Next · Horizon 2

Operating resilience and adoption

Later · Horizon 3

Durability, optionality and debt
