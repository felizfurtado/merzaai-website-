01

Transformation Clarity

When change is busy but stuck

What you may be seeing

High activity, competing priorities, disputed target states, unclear ownership or important executive decisions that keep moving forward without being resolved.

What may be underneath

Teams are progressing against different assumptions. The organisation has not reached a shared view of the problem, the choices or the destination.

What needs to change

A common understanding of the challenge, explicit decisions, a coherent target state and a roadmap that gives people clear direction.

02

Treasury Transformation

When the operating model needs to catch up

What you may be seeing

Limited cash visibility, unreliable forecasting, fragmented banking relationships, unclear decision rights or technology choices shaping how treasury operates.

What may be underneath

Operating models, governance, processes and systems are not working as one. Responsibilities and information flows may not support the decisions leaders need to make.

What needs to change

A treasury model with clearer accountability, stronger cash visibility, fit-for-purpose processes and technology aligned to the way the organisation needs to work.
